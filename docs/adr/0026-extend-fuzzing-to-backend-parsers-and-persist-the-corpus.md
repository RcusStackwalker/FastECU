# ADR 0026: Extend Fuzzing to Backend Parsers and Persist the Corpus

## Status

Proposed. Extends [ADR 0021](0021-fuzz-portable-code-with-fuzztest.md).

## Context

ADR 0021 scopes fuzzing to `src/algorithms` and `src/backend` and names ROM
files, definition XML and terminal scripts among its inputs, yet all seven
harnesses sit in `src/algorithms/protocol` and `src/algorithms/expression`.
`src/backend` has none. Its parsers read user-supplied files and index tables
by file-controlled sizes and offsets, and they already take byte spans or
string views (`ParseRomraiderDefinition`, `ParseRomraiderIndex`,
`ParseLoggerDefinition`, `ConvertMappackCsv`, `ParseTerminalScript`).
`.github/workflows/fuzz.yml` runs every `fuzz`-tagged harness weekly for 10
minutes, but each run starts from the committed seeds only, so coverage found
in one run is discarded.

## Decision

- The goal is memory-safety and undefined behavior under ASan and UBSan, plus
  round-trip properties where an inverse exists. Round-trips assert equality
  only on fields the encoder emits, never whole-document equality.
- Harnesses target parse functions that take bytes or strings. Service layers,
  Qt, the filesystem and transports are not fuzzed. A parser with only a
  path-based entry point gets a bytes or string overload first.
- Targets are ranked by how untrusted the input is:
  1. The definition pipeline: byte-span harnesses for the RomRaider, ECUFlash,
     mappack and logger-definition parsers, a round-trip for
     `definition_writer`, and a structured `UnresolvedDefinition` generator
     for `definition_resolver`, whose index and offset arithmetic raw XML
     rarely reaches.
  2. Bus-facing parsers without harnesses: BIU, Colt CAN and vendor-ext, OBD
     frames, DTC and NRC parsers, `ssm_identify`, `mut_memory`, J2534.
  3. ROM image and checksum bounds contracts, using generated right-sized
     synthetic images.
- Stateful sequence fuzzing of drivers is out of scope.
- The scheduled job keeps its corpus in the GitHub Actions cache, not the
  repository. A crash is minimized by a person and committed as a synthetic
  seed of at most 4 KiB under `testdata/fuzz/`, as ADR 0021 requires.

## Alternatives

- Committing the corpus: grows the repository and invites stock ROM bytes into
  it, which ADR 0021 forbids.
- Fuzzing through service layers with fake filesystems: slower, and failures
  point at the fakes more than at the parsers.

## Consequences

- Backend packages gain `*_fuzz_test.cpp` targets, generated by Gazelle as in
  ADR 0021, and must stay buildable under `--config=asan`.
- A cache-held corpus can be evicted. Seeds stay the durable record of every
  crash that mattered.
- Round-trip and bounds properties will report bugs in the encoders and the
  size checks, not only crashes in the parsers.
