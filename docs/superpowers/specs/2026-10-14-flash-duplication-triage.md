## Cohesive proposed boundaries

1. A bounded K-Line send/read layer with explicit framing IDs, timeout, cancellation checkpoints and optional settle; share individual proven operations, not a configurable protocol engine. Keep TCU block pacing and CRC continuation distinct. Unisia Send/Receive may first share within its own named module because its checkpoints differ from the Hitachi helper.
2. A Denso K-Line kernel framing/data module for MC68/SH7055 BE EF framing and opcodes, separate from the existing Denso BEEF CAN module. Share raw optional exchange beneath it only where checkpoints/value semantics are pinned.
3. Extend existing Mitsubishi CVT common module with its identical pure crypto operations. Hitachi CAN tables differ, so its algorithm calls stay data-bound locally.
4. Extend plan primitives with checked fixed-shape device lookup and pure image/transfer shape validation. Keep exact catalogs, supported operations, upload block sizes, transport, confirmation policy, and validation precedence family-owned.
5. Bootloader-dialect operations may grow by a named bounded OBK probe or best-effort stop only after complete return/log/error behavior is pinned. Keep Connect/Read/Reflash/Write/Execute family orchestration pending the independent-source audit, and preserve all unresolved differences.

## Reference limits

RomRaider `src/main/java/com/romraider/io/protocol/ssm/iso9141/SSMProtocol.java` can corroborate SSM packet construction, not flash retry/timing/erase policy. EcuFlash `ecuflash_flat_5630.bin` contains relevant family/kernel strings, but no corresponding flash function has yet been mapped in `decomp_batch.txt`; that mapping must precede a verdict on accidental differences. Existing qualification records explicitly do not establish hardware proof.
